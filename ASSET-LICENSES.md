## Rules for future assets

Before adding an image, icon, font, texture, 3D model, animation, sound or code snippet, record:
- Exact asset name and repository path
- Creator/rightsholder and original source URL
- Exact licence and version, or written permission
- Required attribution and notices
- Whether modification, commercial use and redistribution are permitted
- Date checked and any restrictions

Do not treat a search result, Pinterest/Motionsites reference, public GitHub repository, AI output, or “free download” label as proof of permission. A general animation idea or interaction can be inspiration; copying another creator's actual artwork, code, animation file or distinctive expression may require permission.

## Important limitations

- This register does not establish legal ownership of the whole repository.
- No full binary-file inventory, commit-by-commit authorship review, or third-party asset licence verification was completed.
- Any unknown item stays Amber until its source and rights are confirmed; replace or obtain permission before public/commercial use if it cannot be verified.
- Keep this register updated whenever assets or dependencies change.
