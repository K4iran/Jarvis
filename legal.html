01 / PRIVACY POLICY — DRAFT

STATUS: TECHNICAL AUDIT REQUIRED

Z.E.R.O. is currently being developed as a personal AI assistant. It is not yet presented as a public multi-user service. The exact information processed depends on which features are used.

Information that may be processed

  • Text entered into the assistant and the conversation context needed to generate a reply.
  • Voice or audio data when voice features are used, and any text produced by speech recognition.
  • Personal preferences, settings, memories and information the user chooses to save.
  • Technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as error and diagnostic data, where enabled by the relevant platform.

Where information may go

The current architecture uses a browser-based dashboard hosted through GitHub Pages, Supabase Edge Functions for backend requests, Gemini for AI responses, and Fish Audio for speech generation. Depending on the feature, relevant request content may be sent to those providers so they can perform the requested service. This list must be updated if the architecture changes.

Local storage and third parties

Some app preferences, conversation state and LifeOS-related information are designed to be stored in browser storage on the device. Browser storage is not the same as a secure cloud account and may be cleared by the browser or accessible to someone with access to the device/browser profile. A full inventory of stored fields and retention behaviour is still required.

Third-party providers process data under their own terms and privacy documentation. Their retention, location, human-review and model-training settings must be checked for the exact product and account tier in use. Do not assume that all providers handle data in the same way.

Purpose and sharing

Information should only be processed to provide requested assistant features, maintain security, troubleshoot faults and meet applicable legal requirements. Z.E.R.O. is not intended to sell personal data. No promise is made here that no provider receives request content: the providers needed to deliver a feature may process it.

Retention, rights and deletion

Retention periods and a complete deletion workflow have not yet been verified. Before any public or multi-user release, the operator must document the lawful basis for processing, retention periods, user access/correction/deletion processes, backups, processor agreements and international transfer safeguards where applicable. Users must not be promised deletion from provider systems unless that capability and process have been confirmed.

02 / TERMS OF SERVICE — DRAFT

STATUS: NOT A FINAL USER CONTRACT

These are proposed principles for a future version of Z.E.R.O.; they do not currently form a solicitor-reviewed agreement.

  • Use Z.E.R.O. lawfully and follow the terms of every third-party service involved.
  • Do not use the app to infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity or other rights.
  • Do not attempt to bypass age, location, payment, safety or access restrictions imposed by a provider.
  • Do not rely on AI output as professional legal, medical, financial, emergency or other high-stakes advice.
  • Do not upload information about another person unless you have a lawful reason and any required permission.
  • Do not interfere with the security, availability or operation of the app or its providers.

Any future public terms must identify the responsible operator, explain service availability and limitations, set out complaint/contact routes, and preserve rights that cannot legally be excluded. A disclaimer cannot override consumer law or a provider's contract.

03 / ACCEPTABLE USE

Z.E.R.O. should not be used to:

  • Plan, facilitate or encourage violence, abuse, exploitation, fraud, cybercrime or other unlawful conduct.
  • Harass, threaten, impersonate, stalk or expose private information about someone.
  • Create or distribute non-consensual intimate content, deceptive impersonations or unauthorised voice clones.
  • Generate or distribute content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights.
  • Evade provider safeguards, access controls, age limits, geographic restrictions or usage limits.
  • Probe, attack or disrupt Z.E.R.O., its hosting, databases or third-party services.

These rules are intended as product safeguards. They do not replace provider acceptable-use policies or applicable law.

04 / AI DISCLOSURE

Z.E.R.O. is an AI-powered assistant. Its text responses are generated by an external AI model; its generated speech may be produced by an external text-to-speech provider. Responses can be incomplete, outdated, biased or wrong, and may sound confident even when mistaken.

AI output should be checked before acting on it, especially where safety, money, health, legal rights or other important decisions are involved. Users should not assume that the assistant has independently verified a claim or completed an action unless the app clearly confirms it.

Any future user-facing version should disclose when users are interacting with AI and when audio is processed or generated, in a clear place before the relevant feature is used.

05 / COPYRIGHT & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Users must only submit or use material they have the rights or permission to use, or that is otherwise permitted by law. This includes text, books, lyrics, music, images, video, software, recordings and voice samples.

AI-generated output is not automatically free of copyright or other rights issues. Output may resemble existing material, and the user/operator remains responsible for checking the intended use and applicable provider terms. Do not request or distribute substantial unauthorised copies of protected works.

Z.E.R.O. branding, code and original interface elements should be kept distinct from third-party brands and franchises. Do not use another company's logos, characters, film dialogue, product branding or imply sponsorship or affiliation without permission. Check open-source licences before redistributing code or dependencies.

Rights in user inputs and AI outputs depend on applicable law, the relevant provider's terms and the facts of each use. This page does not grant a licence to third-party content or promise exclusive ownership of generated output.

06 / DATA & MEMORY

Memory features should save only information needed for a clear user-requested purpose. The user should be able to understand what is saved, review it and request correction or deletion.

The current prototype uses browser-side storage for some data. A complete in-app memory inventory, export function, account-wide deletion process and backup deletion procedure are not yet confirmed. Until those controls are implemented and tested, do not describe them as available.

Before multi-user support, each person's data must be isolated by authenticated identity and enforced server-side. Client-side hiding is not access control. Database permissions and row-level security must be independently tested.

07 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The known architecture currently includes the following services. Their role and terms must be rechecked whenever the implementation or provider plan changes.

  • GitHub Pages / GitHub: hosting and source-code collaboration for the static dashboard.
  • Supabase: backend infrastructure and Edge Functions.
  • Google Gemini API: AI model used to generate assistant responses.
  • Fish Audio: speech-generation service.

Each provider has separate terms, privacy documentation, security practices, age/geographic eligibility rules and service tiers. Z.E.R.O. must comply with the exact terms that apply to the account and product being used. A policy on this page cannot make an ineligible use permitted.

Provider-specific retention, model-training use, human review, data location, subprocessors and deletion capabilities must be verified against current official documentation. No API keys, passwords or other credentials belong in this public page or in client-side code.

08 / SAFETY & LIMITATIONS

  • Z.E.R.O. can misunderstand requests and produce incorrect information.
  • Do not use it as an emergency service or a substitute for a qualified professional.
  • Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, authentication codes or other secrets to ordinary chats.
  • Use microphone and voice features only with appropriate notice and consent from anyone whose voice is captured.
  • Use safeguards appropriate to the age and needs of the actual audience. Provider age restrictions must be checked before enabling a provider or inviting users.
  • Before launch, test security, access controls, abuse handling, incident response, backups and recovery.
